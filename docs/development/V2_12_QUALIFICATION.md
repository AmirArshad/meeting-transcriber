## CachyOS

CachyOS x86_64, kernel 7.3.5-1-cachyos, Hyprland on Wayland, PipeWire 1.6.11 and NVIDIA RTX 4070 driver 620.70.06. The GitHub v2.11.0 pacman archive matched SHA-256 `c0f222b417772230129b7863bff07fc71dee5b6c87ddb94f92996f1e4e7abbd1`. It opened a disposable profile and displayed empty and populated History, including an offline Whisper Small result. All release smoke inputs came from repository speech fixtures. The native picker was the GTK desktop portal and the AppImage ran with its sandbox enabled.

The isolated Secret Service fixture ran an Electron 45.2.3 legacy write, an alpha.11 async migration and restart, and an Electron 45 read of a disposable copy. The keyring used protected `v11` ciphertext. Forced basic storage and missing D-Bus used fixed-key `v10` and the app rejected both. The initial alpha.11 attempt exposed stale exact-version guards, which were corrected before the final build.

- The AppImage accepted WAV, MP3 and Opus through the native GTK picker with Whisper Small and English. All three produced playable 14.23-second copies and recognizable local transcripts. SHA-256 matched each original after the imports. After moving the originals, the rebuilt AppImage reopened and played the saved WAV and displayed its transcript. Reimporting the same basename produced a separate ID and audio path. Deleting that duplicate kept the first meeting and the source checksum.
- A pending record seeded by the v2.11 CLI resumed under the rebuilt AppImage and completed with the same ID. AppImage startup also removed a deliberately abandoned staging directory. The packaged Python ran the meeting-manager regressions, including a subprocess interrupted between final-file copies and a concurrent scan under the metadata lock.
- A damaged WAV and an empty WAV gave the choose-another-file action. Cancelling before saving restored History focus and left the meeting count unchanged. A private 64 KiB filesystem provoked real FFmpeg `ENOSPC` with no staged residue. Retry against a larger private filesystem reused the source and produced decodable audio. The packaged meeting manager returned its storage-full code on a separate 180 KiB filesystem and removed the partial promotion.
- A short audio import queued behind a long active transcription. Cancelling left the saved audio available and retry completed under the same meeting ID. On the rebuilt AppImage, the pending transcript showed **Awaiting transcription** and **View in Activity**, and following the link focused the matching job. Cancelling that job showed **Cancelled**, kept focus within its row and left **Retry** available.

Import and Play remained visible and History had no horizontal overflow. At the native menu's 207% zoom step, History still had no horizontal overflow and Import audio remained visible. Shift+Tab and Tab cycled within the dialog, and Escape returned focus to Import audio.

- The packaged History view showed an imported test meeting without a summary. Clicking **Generate summary** and confirming English started the copied llama.cpp runtime on the RTX 4070. The app saved the completed result, confirmed language, transcript hash and sidecars, then displayed the same summary after restart.

### Subtitle decision

Defer SubRip and WebVTT together. The packaged v2.11 Whisper Small fixture returned one merged segment from 0.0 to 13.8 seconds with the full spoken passage. The saved timestamp was `[00:00 - 00:13]`, and the same truncation appeared in the imported WAV and MP3 transcripts. In packaged Parakeet, 1.570–4.610 seconds became `00:01 - 00:04`. The guided Speakrs and Whisper path merged four transcription segments into one from 0.03097 to 13.76722 seconds, saved as `00:00 - 00:13`. The optional speaker sidecar has a transcript hash, but ordinary and older transcripts lack a matching durable fine-timing source. Accurate exports require preserving caption-scale segments tied to the committed transcript and supplying text formats for older meetings.
